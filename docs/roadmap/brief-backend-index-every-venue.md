## PR A — `fix/index-current`: a reading every day, and a close that always publishes
1. **The provisional reading.** The blob gains, per entity, `provisional`: the running-month step when it clears the entity's floor, `{ month, asOf, value, stepPct, n, lo, hi, thin, venues }` with `value` = the last published close × `exp(step)`, `asOf` = the newest sale in the step's sample, and the band carrying the chain's variance plus the step's bootstrap. Below the floor: `{ month, asOf, n, floor, reason: "below-floor" }`. It is never chained, never a `series` point, never a receipts month, and is replaced when the month closes. `readIndexProvisional(entity, key)` exposes it; the chart route and `/api/v1/index` return it under its own key. INV-7 is unchanged (no series point past the last complete month); a new invariant fails when an entity that published last month has no provisional (or below-floor record) younger than 24 hours.
2. **The venue behind every step.** Each published step and the provisional carry `venues`: identities and sales by venue in the step's sample (an identity sold on two venues counts on both, and the totals say so), so the page can print the sample as a receipt line instead of implying a cross-venue index it is not yet.
3. **The close always publishes.** The core batch (every 6 h; 19 to 20 minutes today of its 75) runs the full `warm-sale-panel` after `Core volume`, then the homepage re-warm and the index invariants (INV-7/11/12/13, `--only=index` or equivalent) in the same job, so no index point publishes without them; the 06:00 indices job keeps benchmarks, the weekly report recompose and the full gate. The close then lands within one core cycle of month end, and identity and character pages refresh four times a day. ⚠️ This supersedes PR C of `brief-backend-search-sets-cadence.md` (the panel at the core cadence), which is withdrawn in its favour; its `--panel-only` mode is not needed. Measure the core job over three `workflow_dispatch` runs on your branch and report it.
4. **β and correlation, honestly.** Compute them on MONTHLY returns against BTC's month-end closes; below 12 aligned months return `null` with the reason, never 0. Same for any stat that cannot be computed.
5. **Tests:** the provisional above and below the floor, its replacement at the close, never in `series`; `venues` totals; the β path with 6 and with 12 months; the new invariant.
6. **Report:** the provisional for `market:total`, `ip:pokemon` and `category:tcg` from an `--out` build (September's if you run before Oct 1 00:00 UTC, else October's, with its `asOf`), the `venues` lines for every published month, and the core runtimes.
