The terminal for tokenized collectibles

Prices, volume, holders, pack and claw economics and a resale index across every venue that tokenizes physical collectibles: graded cards, sealed product, memorabilia and more. Built from on-chain data and venue feeds.

The standard

Every figure carries its window and its source. Anything we cannot defend is withheld and says so. The index publishes with its skew disclosed. Deltas are only printed against days every source has finished reporting. This is why the numbers get quoted rather than argued with.

Who reads it

Collectors deciding where to buy, allocators sizing the category, venue teams benchmarking against each other, and writers covering the market. A weekly report and a public thread go out every Monday, naming venues and their moves.

What we already count

Collector Crypt, Courtyard, Phygitals, Beezie and DYLI, on Solana, Base and Polygon. Marketplace resales, primary and pack spend, holders, listings and floors, item metadata down to the cert number, and the payout side of packs and claws where a venue has shared it.

The opportunity

What a fully counted venue gets

Coverage on Varible is earned by data, not by size. A venue that shares its feeds is measured by the same rules as every other venue, and shows up everywhere the market looks.

01 · A PAGE OF YOUR OWN

Your venue, fully instrumented

KPI strip, a scoped Index Studio with BTC and ETH overlays, 14-day cards, top sales with item art, the items and IPs behind your volume. Refreshed every six hours.

02 · CITABLE NUMBERS

Third-party measured

When Varible says a venue's resale volume grew 114% in a week, it is citable, because the number carries its window and source and was computed the same way for everyone. Your own numbers become something others can quote.

03 · DISTRIBUTION

Named every Monday

The weekly report and the public thread name venues and their moves. Item pages carry buy links back to your listings, so a reader who finds an item on Varible finishes on your marketplace.

04 · EMBEDS AND EXPORTS

Charts you can reuse

Every chart exports as PNG and CSV with a cite line, and every chart embeds. Put your own venue chart, computed by a neutral party, on your site or in your investor update.

05 · ECONOMICS

Your Pack story, told honestly

Spend against payouts, realized odds against stated odds, player tiers and concentration. The page that answers the question every serious buyer asks before they play.

06 · THE INDEX

Shape the benchmark

Your resales move the resale comparables index. The more of your feed resolves to an item identity, the more your market shapes the number the category is measured by.

What readers see when a venue is not counted. A dash where a number should be, "no source" on the coverage matrix, and an exclusion note in player analytics. We label every gap as a data gap, never as a venue fault. Readers still see the blank.

What we need, and what each feed powers

Read-only access to your marketplace and pack data. We adapt to what exists: REST and JSON, a webhook, or contract addresses and events we index ourselves. A history export plus a cursor for new rows is enough to start.

One key

A partner API key with documented rate limits, a status page or webhook for outages, the terms under which we display your data with attribution, and a named technical contact on each side.

Formats we take

  • REST JSON with pagination or a cursor
  • Webhooks for new trades and pulls
  • On-chain: addresses plus ABI, we run the indexer
  • CSV history to backfill, any of the above from then on

Public tiers of ten requests a day cannot run a venue. Our usage is predictable: one history backfill, then incremental reads on a six-hour cycle, plus a daily metadata pass.

How we handle it

Rules we hold ourselves to

The data is yours. These are the commitments that make sharing it safe, and they apply to every venue equally.

Payout wallets under rule R3

Payouts are credited only to wallets that spent into the packs. Partner, vendor and treasury flows from the same wallet never appear, and outbound is shown as an aggregate, never as a wallet list.

Read-only, always

We never write, never trade and never hold a key with write scope. Feeds are pulled on a fixed cycle and stored in our own database.

Attribution on every surface

Your name and a link wherever your data appears, including exports and embeds. Your attribution format, your logo rules.

Withheld beats wrong

A cell with no source says so. Nothing is estimated in your name. If a build fails a completeness or sanity gate, the figure is held and the reason is printed.

Tell us what not to show

If there is data you would rather keep off the page, say so. We will show it as withheld rather than guess, and we would rather know than infer.

A named channel

One technical contact on each side, a shared expectation on uptime, and a heads-up before schema changes in either direction.

From key to live

Day 1

Key received. We pull a sample, check fields and identity resolution, and send back what we found.

Week 1

History backfilled into the spine. Completeness and sanity gates run. Board and venue page go up.

Week 2

Economics and player analytics live where the feeds allow. Your first named appearance in the Monday report.

Ongoing

Six-hour refresh, daily spine, weekly report. Schema changes agreed in advance, both ways.
